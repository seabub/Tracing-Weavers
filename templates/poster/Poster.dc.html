BEYOND TENUN
Seed to Loom

Humanizing Artificial Intelligence through Ancestral Intelligence.

From local resilience to human flourishing.

Three shocks in six years interrupted more than homes in East Nusa Tenggara. They interrupted livelihoods, the transfer of knowledge, identity and opportunity. Beyond Tenun is a three-year pathway that treats the knowledge held in these communities as intelligence worth investing in — beginning with the loom, and ending with people who own their own capacity.
