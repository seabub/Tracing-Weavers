Why this matters

What happens when disaster interrupts livelihoods, knowledge, identity and opportunity?

Livelihood interrupted

Income from land and loom stops while the cost of living carries on.

Knowledge transfer interrupted

Teaching moves to survival. What elders hold is not passed on during a shock.

Identity and opportunity interrupted

People are left to rebuild as beneficiaries instead of as owners of their own capacity.

Field mapping in Adonara, Lembata and Manggarai currently lists 580 active weavers.
How it works

Seed → Loom → Trace → Teach → Regenerate → Hub → Flourish

Restore land, cotton and local ecological knowledge. Strengthen skills, culture and women's livelihood. Connect weaver, product, story, provenance and impact. Build teacher capacity and co-create a locally rooted curriculum. Climate action beyond carbon, linking ecology, livelihoods and culture. A Local Impact Hub for capacity building, incubation and enterprise development. Strengthen agency, dignity, resilience and future opportunity.
WHY THIS ORDER
Each step only holds if the one before it is in place. That is why it is funded as one pathway and not as seven grants.
Seed to Loom is framed as revival and strengthening because the land, buildings, weaving skills and trusted organizations already exist
