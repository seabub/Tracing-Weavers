# BEYOND TENUN
### Restoring Ancestor Intelligence, Reviving NTT's Economy
**Executive Summary — Fundraising Pitch Deck**

---

## THE CHALLENGE

The art of weaving in Flores stands on the edge of extinction — and the window to save it is closing fast.

1. **A dying craft.** Weaving expertise is now held by only **580 elderly women** (250 + 250 + 80) across three weaving communities. Every year without regeneration brings us closer to losing knowledge passed down for centuries, with no way to recover it once it's gone.

2. **Untapped economic value.** Kain Tenun — a high-value cultural heritage — is still treated as a mere commodity today, sold far below its true worth because it has never been marketed to its full potential.

3. **No pathway for regeneration.** The younger generation has virtually zero weaving skills. Without a structured mechanism to transfer this knowledge, the craft will disappear along with the generation that still holds it.

> **The Momentum:** The natural disasters that struck NTT have left deep wounds in these communities — yet it is often from the lowest point that the strongest revival emerges. **BEYOND TENUN** answers this moment: turning post-disaster recovery into a turning point for renewed spirit and economic momentum across NTT, with Tenun as both the symbol and the engine of that revival.

---

## THE BIG IDEA

**BEYOND TENUN is an *ancestor intelligence* restoration program** — reviving weaving expertise as a living asset, systematically passed down to the next generation through two core pathways:

- **Teacher Capacity Building** — training teachers to carry the knowledge forward
- **Tenun as Local Curriculum** — making weaving an official part of formal education

At the same time, the program creates **1,000 local sociopreneurs** from weaving communities — transforming weavers from commodity producers into entrepreneurs who understand and can monetize the true value of their heritage.

## THREE-YEAR JOURNEY

**Year 1 — RESTORE**
Assess communities, recover knowledge from senior weavers, reactivate weaving practices, and establish the program's foundation.

**Year 2 — REGENERATE**
Grow entrepreneurial capacity, enterprise development, formal learning, technology adoption, and climate resilience through cotton and natural dye farming.

**Year 3 — FLOURISH**
Deepen local leadership, achieve program self-sustainability, expand market connections, and spread knowledge to other regions.

## EXPECTED IMPACT AREAS

### 1. Local Economy Growing Stronger with the Community
Weaving communities — dominated by elderly women who are master weavers — have long produced Kain Tenun that is treated as a mere commodity, without recognition of its value as high-worth cultural heritage. **BEYOND TENUN** builds community capacity to transform into sociopreneurs, elevating Tenun from a commodity into a heritage product of high economic value.

### 2. Tenun as a Curriculum for Future Education
Today's younger generation has virtually no weaving skills — a real risk that this *ancestor intelligence* will vanish if it is not passed down soon. This long-term program develops learning modules (covering the history, value, and technique of weaving) in collaboration with local communities and the Education Office (Dinas Pendidikan), followed by teacher training so they can become the bridge that transfers this knowledge and skill to their students — the next generation.

### 3. Reviving Local Cotton & Natural Dyes
Climate issues have halted local cotton production, forcing dependence on imports from China. This program revives local cotton and natural dye farming through collaboration with the Agriculture and Plantation Office (Dinas Pertanian & Perkebunan) — while also opening a new source of livelihood for men in the community (men farm, women weave), creating a complete and sustainable family economic ecosystem.

### 4. Amplifying Ancestor Intelligence Through Traceable Weaver Technology
Until now, the story behind every piece of Kain Tenun — who wove it, which community it came from, and the meaning behind its motifs — has been lost the moment the fabric changes hands in the market. As a result, Kain Tenun is sold without context, and its value is reduced to that of ordinary textile.

**BEYOND TENUN** introduces *Traceable Weaver* technology — digital certification via an embedded **NFC Chip** that directly connects the story from the weaver's hands to the end buyer. This creates a self-reinforcing value cycle:

1. **Restore Weaving/TENUN** — reviving weaving expertise as the foundation
2. **Increase Value** — creating commercial patterns and digital certification to trace authenticity
3. **Increase Awareness & Distribution** — expanding reach to the global market via social media
4. **Strengthen Local Weaver & Upgrade Local Economy** — value flows back to weavers and the local economy
5. **Preserved by NFC Chip** — the story and authenticity of Ancestor Intelligence remain digitally safeguarded
6. **Preserved in Curriculum** — the same knowledge is formally passed down to SMP and SMA students

With this technology, every transaction becomes more than a fabric sale — it becomes an experience directly connected to ancestral heritage, backed by an authenticity guarantee that builds trust and increases market value globally.
