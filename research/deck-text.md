T H E B I G I D E A
Ancestral intelligence is knowledge that has already been tested for
generations: when to plant, which plant gives which colour, what a motif
is allowed to say, who may weave it, how a household survives a bad
season. It is held by people, carried in practice, and transferred by
relationship.
It is data that was never written down, so it
disappears when transfer stops.
Technology is the enabler of this intelligence.
It is not the hero of the program.
Humanizing AI through
Ancestral Intelligence
Communities rebuild not from what they lack, but
