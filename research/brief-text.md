1. Assignment Context
Impact Creative Management (ICM), Transformational Business Network (TBN) and Torajamelo have agreed to collaborate on one pilot program, Beyond Tenun - Seed to Loom, focused on communities in Adonara, Lembata and Manggarai. The partnership intends to use TBN Conference 2026 as a strategic moment to attract serious funding partners for a three-year program and to introduce the ICM ecosystem through a real collaborative case.
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
Create a coherent set of conference materials that helps potential investors, donors, foundations, grant makers and strategic partners understand, remember and engage with Beyond Tenun - and move into a meaningful follow-up conversation.
2. Communication Hierarchy
Priority
What the audience should understand
1. HERO
Beyond Tenun - Seed to Loom is the concrete program and funding opportunity.
2. ECOSYSTEM
ICM × TBN × Torajamelo combine complementary capabilities to make the program possible.
3. CENTER
Local communities, local knowledge, women and local institutions remain at the center.
4. VISION
Humanizing AI through Ancestral Intelligence, ultimately contributing to human flourishing.
5. CTA
Join the three-year journey as an Impact Partner / funding partner / strategic collaborator.
3. Conference Conversion Goal
1. NOTICE - make the booth and materials visually strong enough to stop the right people.
2. UNDERSTAND - explain Beyond Tenun simply within 2-3 minutes.
3. BELIEVE - show credible program logic, collaboration and real community grounding.
4. ENGAGE - create reasons for investors/funders to ask questions and spend time with the team.
5. CONTINUE - capture interest and move qualified contacts into follow-up meetings after the conference.

6. Production &amp; Handover Requirements
All deliverables must use the approved master visual and message system.
Provide final export files plus editable/source files for decks, layouts and graphics wherever technically possible.
Print materials must be supplied in print-ready format with bleed/safe area according to final vendor specifications.
Videos must include a 16:9 HD version and a compressed digital-sharing version.
All content must be reviewed and approved by the ICM/TBN/Torajamelo team before final production.
Agency must not invent impact figures, beneficiary numbers, investor returns or unapproved claims.
Any third-party photography, music, fonts or footage must have proper usage rights.
Agency should use real community/program assets supplied by the team where appropriate, with respectful and non-extractive storytelling.
